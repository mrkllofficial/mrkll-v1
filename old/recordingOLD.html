FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I book a recording session?

Submit a booking request through the website. We'll review your project and confirm the session before payment.

How does Mixing and Mastering work?

Orders are paid online. After payment you'll receive instructions to securely upload your files.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes revisions to ensure the final result meets the agreed artistic vision.

Can I work remotely?

Yes. We work with artists worldwide through a secure online workflow.

Which file formats do you accept?

WAV 24-bit (or higher when available), exported without clipping and with enough headroom.
